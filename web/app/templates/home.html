About

Track Insights

Indiana high school track and field postseason results, built by students for the athletes, coaches and fans who follow them.

Our story

It started with one question

“I wonder how our 4x800 time would place in other Sectionals?”

An athlete asked it after a meet, and nobody could answer without digging through every Sectional result in the state by hand. So a few Park Tudor Computer Science students wrote a program to do it.

That program became Track Insights. Today the site covers every IHSAA Sectional, Regional and State result since 2023, with a dashboard for every athlete and every school, rankings by grade and school size, and the unofficial qualifier lists people check during the postseason. The goal has not changed: promote Indiana high school track and field, celebrate what athletes accomplish, and give athletes, coaches and fans something useful.

History

Four seasons in

  1. 2023 – 24

    Created our first program, which shows how athlete results would place in other Sectionals across the state. Shortly after, we built a tool to generate result percentiles, which Park Tudor used to set fair Varsity Letter standards for each event.

  2. 2024 – 25

    Cal B, Malcolm H, Aashiv J, Justin L, Daniel L, Emily W, Owen W, Allison X designed and created the database and developed multiple data analysis tools. Results were posted on Instagram at @trackinsights.

  3. 2025 – 26

    Arnay G, Justin L, Owen Z designed and created the web application, enhanced existing analysis tools, and developed new tools.

  4. 2026 – 27

    Derek A, Navraj B, Aryav D, Dominic G, Sameer Z are redesigning the website and adding new ways to explore and share results.
