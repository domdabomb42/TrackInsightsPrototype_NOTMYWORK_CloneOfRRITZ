About Track Insights

Track Insights is a student-built site for Indiana high school track and field. It collects every IHSAA Sectional, Regional and State result since 2023 and turns it into something useful: a dashboard for every athlete and school, rankings by grade and school size, unofficial qualifier lists during the postseason, and shareable cards for the results worth remembering. Built by Computer Science students at Park Tudor School for the athletes, coaches and fans who follow the sport.

History

2023 – 24
Our first program showed how a result would place in every other Sectional in the state. A percentile tool followed, which Park Tudor used to set its Varsity Letter standards.
2024 – 25
Cal B, Malcolm H, Aashiv J, Justin L, Daniel L, Emily W, Owen W and Allison X built the database and the first data analysis tools, posting results on Instagram.
2025 – 26
Arnay G, Justin L and Owen Z built this web application and expanded the analysis tools.
2026 – 27
Derek A, Navraj B, Aryav D, Dominic G and Sameer Z are redesigning the site and adding new ways to explore and share results.
