About

Track Insights

Your go-to app for IHSAA track and field playoff performances and data insights. Our mission is to promote Indiana high school track and field, celebrate achievements, motivate athletes, and provide meaningful insights for athletes, coaches, and fans.

It started with one question from an athlete

“I wonder how our 4x800 time would place in other Sectionals?”

Manually checking state-wide results is tedious and error-prone. That question inspired our first project: show how any performance stacks up in every other Sectional across the state. From there, many more ideas emerged, and Track Insights grew into the set of tools you see today, built by Park Tudor Computer Science students.
