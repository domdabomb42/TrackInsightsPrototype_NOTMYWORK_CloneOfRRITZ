About Track Insights

Built by students.
Made for Indiana track and field.

We’re a group of Computer Science students at Park Tudor in Indianapolis, and some of us run track. Track Insights started because we wanted an easier way to see how our results compared with the rest of the state.

Now the site has every IHSAA Sectional, Regional and State result since 2023, with a page for every athlete and school, rankings by grade and school size, qualifier lists during the postseason, and cards you can share when you have a result worth showing off.

  • 23,000+Athletes
  • 62,500+Individual results
  • 8,150+Relay results
  • 400+Member schools
  • 246Postseason meets
  • Every IHSAA Sectional, Regional and State meet since 2023.

What you’ll find

  • 01Athlete dashboards

    Every postseason mark an athlete has, in one place, plus badges for the big ones.

  • 02School dashboards

    How a team’s entries did at each round, and how the program compares with schools its size.

  • 03Rankings & percentiles

    Where a mark lands by grade, by school size, and against the whole state.

  • 04Qualifiers & predictions

    Our unofficial Regional and State qualifier lists during the postseason, and projections based on past years.

How we got here

  1. 2023 – 24

    We wrote our first program to see how a result would have placed in every other Sectional in the state. A percentile tool came next, and Park Tudor used it to set its Varsity Letter standards.

  2. 2024 – 25

    Cal B, Malcolm H, Aashiv J, Justin L, Daniel L, Emily W, Owen W and Allison X built the database and the first analysis tools, and started posting results on Instagram.

  3. 2025 – 26

    Arnay G, Justin L and Owen Z turned it into this website and added more tools.

  4. 2026 – 27

    Derek A, Navraj B, Aryav D, Dominic G and Sameer Z are redesigning the site and working on new ways to look at and share results.
